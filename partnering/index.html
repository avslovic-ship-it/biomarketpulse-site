How this is built

Commitment = 40 points per cyclic or constrained peptide approved since 2026 + 15 per older approval + 12 per clinical program + 12 per deal since 2024 + 4 points per billion dollars of disclosed deal value (capped at 20), capped at 100.

Firepower = up to 40 points for quarterly sales (full marks at $25 billion), up to 30 for growth (−5% to +20%) and up to 30 for adjusted margin (full marks at 30%).

Built from 411 registered clinical trials, about 6,000 papers counted by year, 25 programs, 10 deals since 2024, three 2026 approvals, 15 quarterly reports and 22 share-price series. Original articles and filings are kept in the evidence trail, not linked here.

Limits. Deal values are headline figures. Trial counts include academic studies. Program scaffolds are a draft classification awaiting chemist review. This is market intelligence, not investment advice.
